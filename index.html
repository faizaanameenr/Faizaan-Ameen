A LITTLE ABOUT ME / 02
Curious by
default.

Not just making it work.
Making it make sense.

I’m a technology enthusiast who likes getting close to the whole product: how it looks, how it behaves, how its pieces communicate, and what happens when something goes wrong.

My main focus is Android development and AI-driven software. I also spend time on UI/UX, automation, and the systems around a product, from backend integration to release checks and documentation.

I value evidence over assumptions, root-cause debugging over random fixes, and small changes that preserve what already works.
